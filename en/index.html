About

I am a master's student in the Astrometry Group at Nanjing University, supervised by Niu Liu. I received my B.Sc. in Space Science and Technology from Nanjing University in 2026.

Research Interests

Pulsar Timing Astrometry

I compare pulsar positions, proper motions, and parallaxes across pulsar timing arrays (PTAs) to assess the precision and consistency of timing solutions. I also combine very long baseline interferometry (VLBI) with Gaia astrometry to study how pulsar timing reference frames can be tied to the International Celestial Reference Frame (ICRF).

Stellar Aberration-Based Autonomous Navigation

I use Gaia DR3 to build navigation star catalogs and assess their sky coverage and positional accuracy. I study how catalog errors and uncertainties in plate solutions affect spacecraft velocity estimates.
