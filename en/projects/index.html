Research Projects

As Principal Investigator

Astrometric Systematic Errors in Stellar Aberration-Based Autonomous Navigation for Spacecraft

Funding
National Natural Science Foundation of China, undergraduate research project (125B1029)
Role
Principal investigator

Using Gaia DR3, I evaluated nine plate models across the sky to estimate how plate-solution uncertainty affects velocity estimates from stellar aberration. For the sparse star fields spanning about 1° considered in the study, this uncertainty corresponds to velocity errors on the order of meters per second.
